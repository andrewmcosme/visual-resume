Welcome

My name is Andrew Cosme, and thank you for being here. I call this my “visual resume,” which serves as my portfolio. Upon applying for jobs after college, I noticed a gap in my resume, lacking a portfolio as it is commonly known. I wanted to create a project to fill that gap and showcase my skills in software engineering. But with how common and easy it is to make a site these days, I wanted to do it with a twist, one that showcased my creative side and my belief that AI and technology at large should serve as a tool for humans, not a replacement.

As Steve Jobs, one of my favorite icons and speakers, famously said, “it’s technology married with liberal arts, married with the humanities, that yields us the results that make our heart sing.” So this project harnesses AI to showcase my skillset and provide a visual and creative element to my work history and employment history. You will also get to learn a little bit about me as a human outside of work - because I am more than just an employee or a student.

Thank you for taking the time to read and find out more about the human on the other side of this website. For the purposes of making a statement, none of the “art” or written statements were AI generated - I, Andrew, a human, took the time and effort to draw these visuals and write these paragraphs. So while the art may not look perfect and my writing may be a bit off, I think it says more about me as a human that I took time to make it personal, and make it my own.
